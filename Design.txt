Original Design.txt:

This game is based upon a board game that I made in high school. You play as a 17th century charter 
company which is trying to reach a certain amount of gold or elimate all other charter companies. 
In this iteration, it is single player, so it focuses only on the reaching a gold goal. There are 
many unique mechanics in the full game, but the main mechanic used in this prototype 
is the city sales mechanic. When the player sells an item to a city, it is put in that city's 
inventory. The city will pay a premium for resources that they do not have in their inventory and do 
not produce. The city has a -80% buy rate for items that have just been sold to them, and that effect 
reduces based on the number of turns and items that have passed since this city was sold that item. 
This mechanic forces the player to travel around the map and interact with a lot of cities to find 
the optimal world trading routes as opposed to just dumping all of their resources at one city. The 
main inspiration behind the game way Civilization 6 and its naval troop and trader mechanics. In 
that game, selling resources, trade routes, and naval units are all seperate features, but this game 
intends to combine all of them into one. The prototype aims to make the player come up with their own 
routing strategy by balancing what resources and ships they buy and where they sell to.

Final Iteraton:

There were three main suggestions for how to fix the game:
1. Make the ships be able to move more.
    - This was very easy to fix. All that was done was increase all of the ships movement capablilities 
    by two.
2. Make the goal easier to achieve. 
    - The initial gold amount to get was 200, but it has been changed to 100 to make it more achieveable
3. Prices lose value too fast.
    - Changed the penalty for selling the same item to a place from -80% to just -50% to make it a bit 
    easier for the player 
4. Unclear what was affected when buyong something.
    - Added a sell preview that showed the crash value after you sell an item to a place (eg. 3g -> 1g)
5. Have instructions available while playing
    - Pressing "I" while in game brings up the instructions screen for easy reference.
6. Automated trading paths
    - Added the ability to press "R" and a city which makes the ship auto-sail to teh selected city.
    - Added the ability to press "H" whch makes the ship skip a turn if you don't want it to move
    - Added a screen when end the turn is pressed so that the player doesn't forget to move all ships
